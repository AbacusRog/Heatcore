Privacy policy

Job Margin Checker. Last updated 31 October 2026.

Job Margin Checker is a web app for working out the profit on jobs and for sending quotes and invoices. It is provided by Abacus Consultancy Team Limited (company number 09416420), referred to here as “we”. This page explains what information the app keeps and why.

Who this covers

  • Account holders: the business owner and anyone they approve to sign in.
  • Customers of an account holder: people who receive a quote or invoice from the app.

What we keep

  • Sign-in details: your email address and a securely stored password.
  • Business details you enter: business name, address, email, phone, VAT number, logo, bank details for invoices, and your standard terms.
  • Jobs: costs, materials, labour, extra costs and margins you enter.
  • Quotes and invoices: customer name, email address, address, the work and prices, and whether the customer accepted or declined (including the name they typed to accept).
  • QuickBooks connection (only if you connect it): your QuickBooks company identifier and company name, and the access tokens needed to act on your behalf. Tokens are encrypted before they are stored.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: to show your jobs, quotes and invoices, to let your customers view and answer a quote through a private link, and, when you press the button, to create the invoice in your QuickBooks company. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

QuickBooks

If you connect QuickBooks, the app reads your customers, items and tax codes and creates a customer (if needed) and an invoice when you press Send to QuickBooks. It does not delete anything in QuickBooks and does not look at your other accounting records. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which revokes the connection with Intuit and removes the stored tokens.

Customer links

Each quote or invoice you email has a long private link that only works for that document. Anyone who has the link can view it, so share it only with the customer. Drafts are never visible through a link.

Where it is stored

The app runs on Cloudflare and keeps its data in a Supabase database. Each account can only see its own records. Connections use HTTPS. Our technical providers may process data on our behalf in the UK, EU or elsewhere under their standard data-protection terms.

How long we keep it

For as long as the account is in use. You can delete jobs, quotes and invoices in the app. If you ask us to close an account, we delete its data, except where we must keep something by law.

Your rights

Under UK data-protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how it is used. If you are a customer of one of our account holders, please contact that business first, as they decide what is put on your quote or invoice. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
